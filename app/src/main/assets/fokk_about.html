Defense & Engine

5-Pillar Protection Model

1
Document-Start JSON Sanitizer (YouTube & Instagram)
Hooks YouTube initial player data and Instagram GraphQL connections at document start, deleting ad metadata before the player can initialize. Zero black screen delays and zero anti-adblock detection.
2
On-Device Local Domain Blocklist
Intercepts ad networks, tracking telemetry, and malicious analytics on-device without shipping your browsing requests to remote filter clouds.
3
Built-in WebTorrent & Paywall Mirror Engine
Click any magnet link or .torrent file to stream media directly in your tab at full internet speed. Tap the command bar mirror button to bypass paywalls on news articles instantly.
4
Dual-Deck Split Viewport & Cyber Reader
Multitask with two active independent web pages stacked horizontally on mobile. Activate Cyber Reader for clean, high-contrast, distraction-free reading.
5
Physically Isolated Ghost Profile (fokk_private)
Private tabs operate in an entirely segregated OS directory and process. Storage, indexedDB, and cookies are physically walled off from normal tabs and wiped with zero residuals on exit.
Security Truth

Why No Fake "Free VPN"?

Browsers like Opera advertise a "free VPN," but in reality it is an unencrypted HTTP proxy that routes your data through their servers. Operating global proxy servers costs millions, which free proxies monetize by logging your browsing data and tracking traffic, while causing Cloudflare CAPTCHAs and throttling speeds.

Fokk Shield protects you honestly: We integrate directly with audited, zero-log Encrypted Private DNS (AdGuard, Mullvad, Cloudflare, Control D) to shield your queries from your ISP, perform 100% of ad neutralization on your device, and allow unthrottled line-speed P2P torrent streaming. If you need a full IP tunnel, Fokk Shield works seamlessly with real OS-level VPNs (WireGuard, Mullvad).

Search

Why Ecosia is Default

Fokk selects Ecosia as the default search engine for all new users. Ecosia dedicates 100% of its profits to climate action and tree planting, operates on 200% renewable energy, and respects your privacy by not creating permanent user profiles or selling data to advertisers.

You retain complete control: switch to DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Google, or Startpage at any time via Browser Menu → Search Engine.
