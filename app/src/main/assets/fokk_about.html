Fokk

Version 1.0.72

A fast, privacy-focused Android web browser built for on-device content filtering, background-free media playback, and torrent streaming.

Capabilities

Built for Performance & Privacy

Built from the ground up for speed, low memory usage, and zero background tracking. All content sanitization and blocking happen directly on your device.

1
Document-Start Video & Social Filtering
Intercepts YouTube initial player responses and Instagram network connections at document start, stripping ad metadata before the player initializes. Videos and feeds load cleanly without buffering stalls or blank screen delays.
2
In-Browser WebTorrent & Magnet Streaming
Streams video and audio directly in your browser tabs from magnet links and .torrent files over WebRTC and BitTorrent protocols, without requiring third-party torrent apps.
3
Dual-Deck Split Viewport
Allows running two independent live web tabs stacked vertically on a single mobile screen, enabling simultaneous media playback and article reading.
4
1-Tap Paywall Mirror & Reader Mode
Access clean, archived versions of paywalled articles with a single tap on the address bar, or switch to a distraction-free reader view for long-form reading.
5
Isolated Private Profile (fokk_private)
Private tabs operate in a physically segregated OS directory and process. Storage, cookies, and cache are completely separated from normal tabs and cleared on close.
6
Ergonomic Animated Command Bar
Bottom-anchored navigation card designed for one-handed mobile reach, featuring choreographed fluid expansion when typing and an integrated tab switcher.
7
AMOLED Pure-Black Dark Theme
Hardware-accelerated dark engine that formats web backgrounds to pure #000000 black, optimizing visual contrast and reducing battery consumption on OLED displays.
8
Lightweight Footprint
Installs under 20 MB with near-instant startup, zero background telemetry, and minimal memory usage.
Privacy Architecture

On-Device Security by Design

100% Local Execution: All ad neutralization, script scrubbing, and content filters run locally on your device. Your web requests and browsing history are never routed through third-party proxy servers or analytical intermediaries.

Encrypted DNS: Integrates with encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) and Android Private DNS providers (including AdGuard DNS, Mullvad, Cloudflare, and Control D) to shield domain queries from network-level inspection at full line speed.

Zero Telemetry: Fokk contains zero analytics SDKs, collects no tracking metrics, and performs no background user profiling.

Search

Why Ecosia is Default

Fokk selects Ecosia as the default search engine for all new users. Ecosia dedicates 100% of its profits to climate action and tree planting, operates on 200% renewable energy, and respects your privacy by not creating permanent user profiles or selling data to advertisers.

You retain complete control: switch to DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Google, or Startpage at any time via Browser Menu → Search Engine.
