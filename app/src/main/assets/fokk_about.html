Multi-Layer Defense Architecture
1. DNS-Grade Network Filtering Intercepts network requests before dispatch. Blocks over 120,000 ad servers, analytics beacons, fingerprinters, and surveillance domains at zero CPU cost.
2. Anti-App-Nag & Promo Eradication Eliminates aggressive "Open in app" interstitials and bottom sheets on YouTube, Instagram, Reddit, Twitter/X, and Quora, letting you browse without interruptions.
3. AI Overviews & Slop Filter Hides synthetic search engine filler, non-consensual AI summaries, and sponsored snippets to provide authentic, original web content.
4. Biometric Ghost Mode Profiles Private tabs operate in an isolated memory-only container. Optional hardware biometric lock protects ghost tabs from unauthorized physical access.
Search Engines & Ecosia
Fokk Shield partners with Ecosia as the out-of-the-box search engine for new users. 100% of Ecosia's profits go toward planting trees in deforested regions, their servers run on 200% renewable energy, and searches are never sold to advertisers or used for personal profiling.
Users retain total sovereign control: switch with 1 tap to Brave Search (independent web index), DuckDuckGo, Startpage, or Google at any time.

Privacy Policy & Sandboxing
1. No Collection: Fokk Shield does not collect, log, store, or transmit your search history, URLs visited, IP address, device fingerprints, or browsing behavior.
2. Local Device Sandbox: All browsing data, session tabs, cookies, and local bookmarks reside strictly in your private application storage sandbox (/data/data/com.adshield.app/).
3. Ghost Mode Ephemerality: Ghost Mode tabs execute inside isolated memory without disk persistence. Upon closing a Ghost session, all caches, DOM storage, and session tokens are immediately purged.
